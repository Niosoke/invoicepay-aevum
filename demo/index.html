InvoicePay

Stablecoin invoices on the QDay Aevum testnet (chain 44005). A merchant creates an invoice in tUSD8, the named payer settles the exact amount on chain, and the contract marks it paid. The contract never holds funds: payment goes straight from payer to merchant.

How this demo works

  1. Connect a wallet. The demo adds the QDay Aevum testnet for you.
  2. As the merchant, create an invoice naming the payer and the amount.
  3. As the payer (switch account in your wallet), approve tUSD8 and pay.
  4. Watch the invoice list below flip from Open to Paid, with explorer links for every transaction.

You need testnet tQDAY for gas and tUSD8 to pay with. Both come free from the faucet at faucet.qday.info. This is a testnet: nothing here has real value.

1. Connect

The contract address below is the live deployment. You only need to change it if you deploy your own copy.

2. Merchant: create an invoice

You are the merchant. Name the one wallet that is allowed to pay this invoice, set the amount in tUSD8, pick a due date, and add a memo your payer will recognise.

3. Payer: pay an invoice

Switch to the payer account in your wallet first. Paying takes two wallet confirmations: one to approve the contract to spend the invoice amount of tUSD8, then one to pay. Only the named payer can pay, and only once.

4. Invoices on chain

Read live from the contract. Refresh after creating or paying an invoice.
